✨ Pro Gesture Tip: Menu Swipes

In the Main Menu, you can swipe the “To-Do Lists / Checklists” row to instantly create a specific checklist type:

👉 Swipe Left → Right: Launches a Quick Checklist with live Day, Date & Last Edited stamp.
👈 Swipe Right → Left: Launches a Spatial Canvas Checklist with sticky notes & visual arrows pointing to items.
👆 Tap & Hold any item/card: Opens the iOS 27 Liquid Glass context menu & Creation Time info.
